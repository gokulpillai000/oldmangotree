# The Monsoon Rendezvous: A Short Story

The rain did not merely fall; it possessed the courtyard with the familiarity of a forgotten relative returning without announcement.

Beneath the aged wooden eaves, the scent of damp clay stirred memories of monsoons gone by, each droplet carrying fragments of unresolved conversations.
